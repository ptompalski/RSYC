# RSYC: Remote Sensing-Derived Yield Curves for Canada

Provides published remote sensing-based yield curves for aboveground
biomass and total volume across Canada, as described in Tompalski et al.
(2024)
[doi:10.1016/j.foreco.2024.121894](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2024.121894)
and Tompalski et al. (2025)
[doi:10.1093/forestry/cpaf067](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpaf067)
. Models are available for 150 km tiles and four levels of Canada's
ecological land classification. Functions help users find curves by
species and area, estimate yield at a given stand age, calibrate curves
to local observations, and build spatial yield-curve datasets.
