# RSYC: Remote Sensing-Derived Yield Curves for Canada

Provides published remote sensing-based yield curves for aboveground
biomass and total volume across Canada. Models are available for 150 km
tiles and four levels of Canada's ecological land classification.
Functions help users estimate yield at a given stand age and find curves
by species and area.
